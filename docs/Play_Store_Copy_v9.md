Title: **Save The Baby: Match 3 Rescue**. Owner-approved; no uniqueness or trademark-clearance claim is made.

Short description:

Match clues, unleash boosters and follow David's adventure to rescue baby Ethan.

Full description:

Ethan has vanished. Help David and Amara follow the evidence in a story-driven match-3 adventure.

MATCH CLUES AND CLEAR THE WAY

Swap neighboring pieces, complete objectives and clear blockers as the investigation unfolds. Create Rockets, Bombs and Thunder specials to help with difficult boards.

MAKE EVERY TOOL COUNT

Earn supplies through normal play and reward challenges. Tray boosters preserve your remaining moves. Return to earlier levels with the tools you have learned and practise special matches for the challenges ahead.

FOLLOW THE SEARCH FOR ETHAN

Explore changing locations, make story choices and collect evidence along the search. Rare Ethan wildcard tiles can complete matches and add extra moves.

ENJOY YOUR REWARDS

Collect daily rewards and take on earned image-restoration challenges. Watch remaining moves and board boosters turn into a celebration after a win.

PLAY WITH THE HELP YOU NEED

Guided lessons introduce new mechanics. Hints and tutorials can be switched off or restored in Settings.

KEEP YOUR PROGRESS

Progress saves on your device. Optional Google sign-in connects cloud saves. The game contains ads and optional in-app purchases.
