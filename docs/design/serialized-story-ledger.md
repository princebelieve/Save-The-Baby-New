## Opening movement ? levels 1?50

### opening_story ? before level 1
Rain, the missing infant, Amara seeing Victor, locked exits, the plea to David. End on the three approaches. Do not explain the inheritance, passage or accomplice.

### chapter01 ? before level 1
Each approach begins with a different first action. Establish where Amara saw Victor; preserve her account without inventing a recording of the ransom call.

### season01 ? after level 5
The external gate was not forced. An innocent delivery worker can be excluded. The house itself becomes the question.

### chapter02 ? before level 9
Victor demands money. Amara denies he has any claim. David must ask where that supposed debt comes from; defer the full answer.

### season02 ? after level 10
Reveal that Ethan inherited a private provision from his father. Victor is not that father. Keep the identity and death history incomplete.

### season03 ? after level 15
A threatening message includes a detail from inside the house. Its sender knew more than a casual observer; establish a source worth testing.

### chapter03 ? before level 16
Amara recalls paying the rent while Victor spoke of his aura. One brief domestic memory explains his habit; avoid narrating the entire relationship.

### season04 ? after level 20
A planted trace is detected before the team acts against its apparent owner. The player protects an innocent person rather than simply losing a lead.

### chapter04 ? before level 23
David admits his first technical interpretation was wrong. Amara identifies a household routine absent from the logs. Their skills complement each other.

### season05 ? after level 25
Amara acknowledges her suspicion that Victor was involved in the father's death, then explicitly distinguishes fear from evidence. No killer is named.

### season06 ? after level 30
A verified witness places a service vehicle near the house. Preserve the witness and record; do not immediately make the witness disappear.

### chapter05 ? before level 31
Victor learns David is helping. Reveal that he approached Amara after the funeral and she refused him. Show jealousy rather than a second aura lecture.

### season07 ? after level 35
The vehicle record leads to an estate service arrangement. First hint of the wealthy family's continuing influence; no full conspiracy disclosure.

### season08 ? after level 40
Someone tries to persuade Amara that she misunderstood what she saw. Physical details support her account. She truly saw Victor; the game will not reverse this into hallucination.

### chapter06 ? before level 41
A poor image from inside the property can be restored. Establish which part matters before the player undertakes restoration. The image is evidence of a route, not a promised rescue.

### season09 ? after level 45
The restored image places Victor beside an interior passage. It corroborates his presence without yet explaining how the closed house worked.

### season10 ? after level 50
An access record bears Amara's credential while she was asleep. End the first fifty levels on this specific locked-house question, leading directly to episode 1.
