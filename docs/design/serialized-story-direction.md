# Save The Baby ? serialized campaign direction

This direction supersedes the rescue ending in The Fortune He Claimed. Ethan is eventually rescued, but not during levels 1?1050. The ending must not announce a rescue, reunite him with Amara, or disguise another child/recording as a completed rescue.

## Audience contract
Players may reasonably believe a rescue is approaching because they gain verified, lasting advantages: proof of life, a named carer, lawful access, a trustworthy witness, a narrowed location and an understood escape route. Characters can hope; the interface must not promise ?rescue Ethan on the next level.? A near miss must have an established cause. Never erase a verified discovery merely to extend the campaign.

## Serial structure
The first 50 levels introduce the locked-house disappearance and only fragments of Amara's past. The next 1,000 levels contain 100 ten-level episodes in ten larger arcs. Each episode closes a smaller problem while moving the rescue mystery. Relationships, conflicting loyalties and the inheritance dispute sustain the drama between discoveries. The protagonist's apparent expertise is tested; Amara supplies knowledge he lacks. Victor's aura is a boast, not confirmed magic.

## Author-only truths and reveal order
Amara left Victor rather than agreeing to exploit Adrian. Adrian, a wealthy man from a royal household, promised marriage; his family opposed it. He acknowledged Ethan and left him a private provision, independently of the disputed throne. His death is suspicious, but suspicion against Victor is not proof. Victor demanded a share after Amara fell in love with David. Amara truly saw Victor carrying Ethan: the later locked-house explanation must vindicate her sighting, not turn it into a dream or hallucination.

Creative supporting names are provisional: Adrian (father), Lady Orena (his mother), Kellan (estate administrator and royal cousin), Nessa (records clerk), Lami (former attendant). They are fictional additions to the creator's foundation. Their roles and guilt must emerge gradually; identifying a payment is not proof of murder. Do not automatically use a core character's portrait for one of these people.

A former service route and misused household access explain the apparent locked-room impossibility. The family supplied a misleading transfer instruction; Victor exploited it for ransom. A frightened intermediary initially helps, then cooperates. Later, different people within the scheme take custody from Victor, changing the search rather than moving the same villain between 100 empty rooms. The infant receives care; the game does not repeatedly endanger him as punishment for failed boards.

## Scene ledger format
The companion episode ledger gives the player-visible setup, lasting discovery, emotional dialogue and next unresolved question for every ten-level episode. These are authoring beats, not five monologues to show simultaneously. Intro shows only what is already known. The discovery belongs near the episode end. Outcome dialogue follows verification. The hook is the next question, not a promise of an imminent rescue.

Within an episode, missions progress through: observe, protect a source, recover context, compare accounts, overcome obstruction, establish the discovery, test an alternative, secure the evidence, make an informed choice, and resolve the local problem. Vary the actual puzzle/story tasks; do not repeat those words as boilerplate dialogue.

## Routes and state
Police: an organised search, witness protection, documented intervention. Signal: technical relationships and the limits of digital evidence. Alone: direct observation and the cost of acting without nearby help. Route-specific information can arrive in a different order; common scenes must use only facts all branches have earned. Invite and acknowledge police help explicitly if a solo player changes approach. Choices change a bounded advantage or relationship, never silently rewrite established history.

Record per scene: required facts, new facts, character knowledge, chosen approach, relationship change, open thread and scheduled payoff. Store all prior factual wins in the case file. Never make a witness forget a confession or restore a revoked access route without an explained new event.

## Cliffhanger variety
Alternate an imminent decision, a relationship rupture, a conflicting document, a live witness, a discovered lie, an ally in danger, a partial antagonist defeat and a new interpretation of a known clue. Reserve physical near-contact with Ethan for major arc endings. Do not end every episode with an empty room.

## Level 1050 boundary
End with a recent independently verified proof of life, the named receiving authority, and a specific way to challenge the transfer holding Ethan away from Amara. She hears a genuine live response from her child, but no physical reunion occurs. The player has obtained a concrete next operation, not another anonymous address. The rescue itself belongs to future content.

## Relationship to gameplay
Defeating blockers opens a physical or informational route. Restoring a picture reveals a detail whose significance was established in conversation. Evidence tiles stand for the episode's investigation, not 30 identical physical documents. A lost board pauses progress; it does not kill a witness or make Ethan's condition worse. Seasons are dramatic arcs rather than a real-time timer; 1,050 puzzle boards are not 1,050 days of infant captivity.

No Atlantis characters, myths, dialogue or plot events are copied. The reference is sustained ensemble drama, overlapping secrets and local victories within a longer central quest.
