Save The Baby Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how S.B. Ibhadode Technologies handles information when you use the current release of Save The Baby (the “Game”).

Who is responsible

The Game is developed and published under the S.B. Ibhadode Technologies brand. For privacy questions, contact info@sbi.globalcreest.com or visit https://sbi.globalcreest.com.

Information stored on your device

The Game does not ask you to create an account or provide your name, phone number, location, contacts, photos, or other personal details. It stores game information locally in the Game’s private app storage so it can remember your progress and preferences. This can include completed and unlocked levels, story choices, tutorial and case-journal progress, in-game coins, lives and boosters, and audio volume and on/off settings.

Campaign progress and inventory are not uploaded to our servers. The Game has no online account or cloud-save feature. The Android release uses internet and network-state access to request advertisements through Google AdMob.

Advertising and consent

The Game integrates Google Mobile Ads and Google's User Messaging Platform. Full-screen advertisements may appear at the end of a level. Optional rewarded advertisements offer the gameplay benefit shown before you choose to watch. Advertisement availability depends on network access and the advertising service.

Google's advertising SDK may collect and share IP addresses (which can indicate approximate location), device and account identifiers such as the Android advertising ID, app and advertisement interactions, and diagnostic information for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. Advertising information is processed by Google and its advertising partners under their policies; it is separate from the campaign progress stored locally by the Game. See https://policies.google.com/privacy and https://developers.google.com/admob/android/next-gen/privacy/play-data-disclosure.

Where required, the Game displays Google's consent message before requesting advertisements. When Google requires a privacy-options entry point, AD PRIVACY OPTIONS appears in the Game's settings so you can revisit your choices. You can also manage your advertising ID using Android's privacy settings. The Game does not request access to precise GPS location, contacts, photos or microphone for advertising.

Information you send to support

If you email info@sbi.globalcreest.com, S.B. Ibhadode Technologies receives the email address and the information you choose to include, and uses it to respond and provide support. The email service provider used to deliver that message also processes it. Support messages are kept only as long as needed to handle the request and maintain appropriate business records, unless a longer period is required by law.

Storage, security, and deletion

Game progress and preferences remain in the app’s private storage on your device. The Game does not keep a server-side copy. You can remove local data by clearing the Game’s storage in Android settings or uninstalling the Game. The Android build does not enable app-data backup. Support email records can be deleted by contacting the email address above, subject to records we must retain by law.

We rely on Android’s app sandbox to protect local files. No method of storage or communication is completely secure, so keep your device and email account protected.

Children

The Game has no account system. Advertising services process information as described above. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the Game or support, contact info@sbi.globalcreest.com so we can review the request and take appropriate action.

Changes to this policy

We update this policy when the Game or its information handling changes. The effective date above will change when the policy is revised.
