# Booster counting and character rules

Tap the Ethan tray entry once to auto-place a stored gift. The search prioritizes a match with two objective clues, then an objective match reachable in one legal swap, then proximity to a single clue. With one move left, it can prefer an immediate ordinary match that earns relief over a placement that merely sits nearby. It preserves existing specials, blockers, Victor and an existing Ethan, and does not consume inventory when no valid placement exists. Search is read-only. Earned Ethan uses the same placement strategy.

An Ethan consumed as part of a wildcard match grants five moves once, before end-of-turn failure is evaluated. This applies equally to earned and gifted Ethan; merely destroying Ethan in a blast grants no move reward. Last-move automatic placement is covered through the real tray-button handler, HUD move signal, inventory deduction and life-loss checks.

Tray and board boosters share `_special_targets`: Rocket clears its row and column, Bomb clears a 3×3 area, and Thunder clears every tile of the chosen type. Swipe either direction to fire a board piece; choose the target by tapping for a tray booster. Reinforced legacy seals accept the same Rocket/Bomb/Thunder blast from either source, including chained specials. Tray use preserves moves; a board swap spends one move. The board special itself is consumed in addition to its targets, without repeating its original collection credit.

Booster swaps resolve the union of their blast and the ordinary matches made by the swap before gravity runs. Each cleared tile contributes at most once in that wave. A piece retained as a new special has already contributed its original collection credit and cannot contribute that credit again when fired. Five in a straight line creates Thunder when that powerup is available, including when a booster swap also makes the five-match. Its creation has visible feedback.

Ethan and Victor are excluded from all random board-generation and refill pools. Their rules start at level 24, where the character tutorial appears. A turn with at least three ordinary match waves earns one Ethan wildcard. Ethan joins at least two ordinary pieces of the same type to form a horizontal or vertical match. A match completed on arrival resolves immediately. A wildcard contributes one collection credit; at intersections, the level's primary collection target takes priority. A gift held in the tray places this same wildcard on an ordinary unblocked tile without spending a move. Gift placement cannot replace blockers, existing specials, Victor, or an existing Ethan, and rejected placements consume no inventory.

Victor appears once per attempt after at least 75% of the configured move budget is spent, provided a move remains. He can be swapped with an adjacent unblocked piece even without a match. This costs one move and replaces all tiles with a playable board using a refreshed seed. Blocker positions and durability remain unchanged. The replacement grants no collection credit, score, character rewards, or inventory specials. Victor does not return after being used or cleared.

Run `powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File tools/run_checks.ps1 -Suite character_booster_checks` for deterministic regression checks. The fixtures use the production swap, match finder, gravity, collection and refill code with presentation delays disabled. The cascade fixture controls refill pieces to produce a reproducible three-wave chain. Coverage includes reverse-direction rocket swaps, immediate matches outside the blast column, overlapping five-matches, retained-special credit, wildcard matching and validation, random-pool exclusion, real cascade rewards, Victor's threshold, one-use limit, move cost, tile replacement and blocker preservation.

Also run the release, level48 and progression_daily suites. These automated checks do not replace device playtesting or certify the new difficulty balance.
