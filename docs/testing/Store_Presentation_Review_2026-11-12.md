# Store presentation review — 12 November 2026

Inspected the first four screenshots downloaded from the live Play listing. They show levels 48, 8, 16 and 31 respectively, each as a static opening board with zero collected objectives. They repeat the same composition rather than showing four distinct player benefits. There are no visible booster blasts, cascade celebrations, story choices or restoration rewards in these first four images. Some tile backgrounds are visibly inconsistent (white/black squares), and this art should be corrected rather than disguised with marketing overlays.

Replace this sequence with actual current-game captures: a clear mid-match board; a Rocket/Bomb/Thunder activation; an Ethan wildcard reward; story characters/location; victory bonus animation; and an earned restoration reward. Keep the playable board dominant in the first three. Use short, readable captions such as Match the clues, Unleash powerful boosters and Bring Ethan home. Do not depict invented gameplay.

No replacement screenshots or video were generated in this review.

## Copy and naming correction

The reviewed Candy Crush Saga, Royal Match and Homescapes listings use broad language about thousands of levels. That does not establish a reason to advertise this game's exact current count. Removed the exact count from the local listing draft. The earlier suggested Save The Baby: Match 3 Rescue rename is withdrawn. Its exact naming collision has not been independently verified, and no candidate should be advertised as unique based on an ordinary search alone.
