APP NAME
Save The Baby

SHORT DESCRIPTION
Solve match-3 puzzles, gather clues, and help David search for baby Ethan

FULL DESCRIPTION
Ethan has vanished, and David must follow the trail. Save The Baby is a single-player, story-driven match-3 adventure that brings puzzle challenges and an unfolding investigation together.

MATCH PIECES AND GATHER CLUES
Swap adjacent pieces, make matches, and work toward each level's objectives. Collect evidence, clear blockers, and plan your moves carefully.

FOLLOW THE SEARCH FOR ETHAN
Join David and Amara as they follow the clues left by Victor. Make story choices and explore the campaign through changing locations, from the village to the forest and factory.

EXPLORE 50 PUZZLE LEVELS
Take on the opening campaign with different objectives, obstacles, and limited-move challenges. Choose useful matches and create special pieces to help clear the board.

LEARN AND USE BOOSTERS
Discover rockets, bombs, and thunder through guided lessons. Use your supplies to tackle challenging boards and review what you have learned in the case journal.

KEEP YOUR PROGRESS
Your campaign progress and preferences are saved on your device, so you can return to the search later.
