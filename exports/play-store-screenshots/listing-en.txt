APP NAME
Save The Baby

SHORT DESCRIPTION
Solve match-3 puzzles, gather clues, and help David search for baby Ethan

FULL DESCRIPTION
Ethan has vanished, and David must follow the trail. Save The Baby is a single-player, story-driven match-3 adventure that brings puzzle challenges and an unfolding investigation together.

MATCH PIECES AND GATHER CLUES
Swap adjacent pieces, make matches, and work toward each level's objectives. Collect evidence, clear blockers, and plan your moves as new challenges appear.

FOLLOW THE STORY
Join David in the search for Ethan. Make story choices, explore the campaign, and follow the investigation through its changing locations.

LEARN AND USE BOOSTERS
Discover rockets, bombs, and thunder through guided lessons. Use your supplies to help with challenging boards and follow your discovered information in the case journal.

KEEP YOUR PROGRESS
Your campaign progress and preferences are saved locally on your device. Clearing app storage or uninstalling the game may erase that progress.
