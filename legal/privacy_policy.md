# Save The Baby Privacy Policy

**Effective date: 2 November 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how S.B. Ibhadode Technologies handles information when you use the current release of Save The Baby (the “Game”).

## Who is responsible

The Game is developed and published under the S.B. Ibhadode Technologies brand. For privacy questions, contact **info@sbi.globalcreest.com** or visit https://sbi.globalcreest.com.

## Information stored on your device

You can play as a guest without creating an account. The Game does not request your phone number, precise location, contacts, or photos. It stores game information locally in the Game’s private app storage so it can remember your progress and preferences. This can include completed and unlocked levels, story choices, tutorial and case-journal progress, in-game coins, lives and boosters, and audio volume and on/off settings.

The Android release uses internet and network-state access for advertising and, when configured and you choose to sign in, account services, cloud saves and purchase verification.

## Optional Google login and cloud saves

When online services are available, you may sign in with Google to back up progress and use it on another device. Google handles your password; we never receive it. The sign-in requests OpenID Connect identity and profile information only. It does not request your email address or access to Gmail. Our server verifies Google's identity token, stores a hashed identifier derived from your Google account ID and your display name, and issues a game session. Login attempts and temporary browser cookies expire after about five minutes; game sessions expire after 30 days or when revoked. Session credentials are kept in the Game's private storage so you can stay signed in.

When signed in, the Game sends campaign progress, story choices, journal/tutorial progress and earned inventory to our backend hosted on Render, with account data stored in MongoDB Atlas. This enables cloud backup and loading on another device. Purchased coins and transaction records are managed on the server. Offline guest progress stays on the device. Upload conflicts are reported instead of silently replacing newer cloud progress.

## Purchases

When purchases are enabled, Google Play handles payment and payment-method details. We receive purchase identifiers/tokens and product information to verify purchases, maintain the purchased coin balance, prevent duplicate credits, fulfill supply purchases, and handle refunds. We do not receive your card number. Google processes purchases under its own policies. Paid coins cannot be redeemed for cash or transferred outside the Game.

## Advertising and consent

The Game integrates Google Mobile Ads and Google's User Messaging Platform. Full-screen advertisements may appear at the end of a level. Optional rewarded advertisements offer the gameplay benefit shown before you choose to watch. Advertisement availability depends on network access and the advertising service.

Google's advertising SDK may collect and share IP addresses (which can indicate approximate location), app and advertisement interactions, and diagnostic information for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. Advertising information is processed by Google and its advertising partners under their policies; it is separate from the campaign progress stored locally by the Game. See https://policies.google.com/privacy and https://developers.google.com/admob/android/privacy/play-data-disclosure.

Where required, the Game displays Google's consent message before requesting advertisements. When Google requires a privacy-options entry point, AD PRIVACY OPTIONS appears in the Game's settings so you can revisit your choices. The Game does not request access to precise GPS location, contacts, photos or microphone for advertising.

## Information you send to support

If you email **info@sbi.globalcreest.com**, S.B. Ibhadode Technologies receives the email address and the information you choose to include, and uses it to respond and provide support. The email service provider used to deliver that message also processes it. Support messages are kept only as long as needed to handle the request and maintain appropriate business records, unless a longer period is required by law.

## Storage, security, and deletion

Guest progress and local backups remain in the app's private storage. Signed-in progress also has a server-side copy as described above. Clearing the Game's storage or uninstalling it removes local data. The Android build does not enable app-data backup. To delete your online profile, cloud save, sessions and purchased balance, use Settings > Account & Coins > Delete Online Account. If you cannot access the Game, email support with your Player ID; we may request proof of ownership. Purchase and debit records needed to prevent replay/fraud or meet legal obligations may be retained after profile deletion. Support email records can be deleted by contacting support, subject to records we must retain by law.

We rely on Android’s app sandbox to protect local files. No method of storage or communication is completely secure, so keep your device and email account protected.

## Children

Before requesting ads, the Android game asks for an age group, including an option not to say. Only the selected category is saved on the device; it is not uploaded to our server. All players receive child-directed advertising treatment with a maximum ad content rating of G. These settings are applied to ad requests. The Android release includes the `AD_ID` and `ACCESS_ADSERVICES_*` permissions declared by the Google Mobile Ads SDK. Age-group answers do not remove gameplay features, ads, optional sign-in or purchases.

Optional sign-in, cloud saves, purchase verification and advertising services process information as described above. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the Game or support, contact **info@sbi.globalcreest.com** so we can review the request and take appropriate action.

## Changes to this policy

We update this policy when the Game or its information handling changes. The effective date above will change when the policy is revised.
