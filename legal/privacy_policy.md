# Save The Baby Privacy Policy

**Effective date: 2 November 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how S.B. Ibhadode Technologies handles information when you use the current release of Save The Baby (the “Game”).

## Who is responsible

The Game is developed and published under the S.B. Ibhadode Technologies brand. For privacy questions, contact **info@sbi.globalcreest.com** or visit https://sbi.globalcreest.com.

## Information stored on your device

The Game does not ask you to create an account or provide your name, phone number, location, contacts, photos, or other personal details. It stores game information locally in the Game’s private app storage so it can remember your progress and preferences. This can include completed and unlocked levels, story choices, tutorial and case-journal progress, in-game coins, lives and boosters, and audio volume and on/off settings.

The current release does not transmit this local game information to S.B. Ibhadode Technologies or another service. It has no online account, cloud-save, advertising, or analytics feature. The Android release does not request network or personal-data permissions.

## Information you send to support

If you email **info@sbi.globalcreest.com**, S.B. Ibhadode Technologies receives the email address and the information you choose to include, and uses it to respond and provide support. The email service provider used to deliver that message also processes it. Support messages are kept only as long as needed to handle the request and maintain appropriate business records, unless a longer period is required by law.

## Storage, security, and deletion

Game progress and preferences remain in the app’s private storage on your device. The Game does not keep a server-side copy. You can remove local data by clearing the Game’s storage in Android settings or uninstalling the Game. The Android build does not enable app-data backup. Support email records can be deleted by contacting the email address above, subject to records we must retain by law.

We rely on Android’s app sandbox to protect local files. No method of storage or communication is completely secure, so keep your device and email account protected.

## Children

The Game has no account system and does not request or transmit personal information from players. If you believe a child has sent personal information to support, contact us at **info@sbi.globalcreest.com** so we can review and delete it where appropriate.

## Changes to this policy

If a later release adds online services, advertising, analytics, accounts, or other data processing, this policy will be updated before those features are introduced. The effective date above will change when the policy is revised.
